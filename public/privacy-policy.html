Personal Assistant Privacy Policy

Personal Assistant is a local-first app for tasks and personal finance. This page explains what it stores and where. Last updated 18 November 2026.

Data stored on the device

Tasks, expenses, savings, debts, wallets, mess records, Cycle records, receipt attachments and optional cached contacts are stored locally on the device or in browser storage. The app does not add its own encryption to this working data. On a phone it sits in the app's private storage; in a browser it sits in that site's localStorage.

Accounts and analytics

Personal Assistant requires no account and uses no advertising or analytics SDK. No app server receives your records.

Location

Emergency SOS requests foreground location only after you choose Share current location. Personal Assistant does not background-track, store or upload location. Calling emergency services does not guarantee that they receive your location.

Contacts

Contacts are read only after permission and a manual Refresh action. Cached names, phones, emails and photo URIs remain local and may be included in backups.

Backups and exports

Encrypted backups use AES-256-GCM and a passphrase-derived key. Plain-text exports are readable and include a warning. Anyone with an encrypted backup needs the passphrase to open it.

Google Drive

Google Drive backup is optional and stays off until you connect your own Google account in Settings. When you use it:

  • The app asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets it see the backup files it created and nothing else in your Drive.
  • Each backup is encrypted on your device with your passphrase before it is uploaded. The upload goes straight to your Google account. The developer of this app never receives it.
  • The sign-in token is kept in the browser tab's session storage on the web, or in the phone's secure key store, and is removed when you disconnect.
  • Automatic backup is off by default. If you turn it on, the backup passphrase is saved in the phone's secure key store so the backup can run, and is erased when you turn it off.

Google's own privacy policy covers what Google does with your account and files.

Deletion

You can delete records, restore supported records from Trash, or erase app data. Shared or downloaded backup files remain outside app control.

Policy updates

If the app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released.

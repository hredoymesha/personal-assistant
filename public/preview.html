Personal Assistant

A local-first app for tasks, expenses, bills, savings, debts, wallets and shared mess accounts. Your records stay in your browser or on your phone. There is no account, no ads and no analytics.

This is a test build, not a finished release. Expect rough edges, and don't keep your only copy of anything important in it.

Open the web appRuns in this browser. Tap Load sample data on Home to look around.

Still in testing

  • The Android app has been tried on one phone so far.
  • Google Drive backup isn't set up in this preview. Encrypted backup files work.
  • Contacts, SOS calling and location sharing only work in the Android app.
  • The web version needs a connection when you reload it.

Your data

  • Browser data and phone data are separate. Move data between them with an encrypted backup file.
  • Data saved in the browser is not encrypted. Backups are, with a passphrase only you know.
